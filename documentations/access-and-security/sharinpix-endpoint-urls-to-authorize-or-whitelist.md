# SharinPix Endpoint URLs to authorize or whitelist

SharinPix uses several endpoints to upload, display and manage your images.

If you have security restrictions, which are typical for certain corporate networks, you may need to whitelist those URLs.

Here is the list of URLs to authorize:

* p.sharinpix.com
* app.sharinpix.com
* api.sharinpix.com
* san.cloudinary.com
* res.cloudinary.com
* api-de.cloudinary.com
* d1qo205ebapjj2.cloudfront.net
* d2ductvi7yot9v.cloudfront.net
