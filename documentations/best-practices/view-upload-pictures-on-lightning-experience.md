# View / Upload Pictures on Lightning Experience

SharinPix uses Lightning components to integrate with Salesforce Lightning Experience.

![](<../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_2020-11-28_at_18.11.37 (1).png>)

To access the SharinPix Lightning components, \*\*My Domain \*\*should be configured for your organization.

Check [here](https://github.com/SharinPix/documentation/blob/main/documentations/getting-started-with-sharinpix/basic-setup-step-3b-for-lightning-users-setup-sharinpix-for-lightning-experience.md) for more details on how to make this configuration.

These Lightning components can be added:

* to a [record Page](../features/main-integration/using-on-a-lightning-record-page.md) or a [home Page](../features/main-integration/using-on-the-lightning-home-page.md)
* as a [Lightning Action](../features/main-integration/using-on-lightning-with-sharinpix-album-lightning-action.md) (so that you can use a button to open a SharinPix Album)
* in [Flows](images-features-in-your-salesforce-flow.md)
