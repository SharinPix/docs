# Copy/Paste

SharinPix allow you to copy and paste in the same album, or from one album to another.

* Select the image(s) to be copied
* Click on the top left button and click on "Copy" or do Ctrl + C from your keyboard

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image - 2026-02-05T164652.106.png>)

* In your destination album, click on an empty area and do Ctrl + V from your keyboard

{% hint style="warning" %}
Ensure that proper permissions has been set for copying and pasting.
{% endhint %}
