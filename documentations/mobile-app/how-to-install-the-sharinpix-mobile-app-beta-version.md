# How to install the SharinPix mobile app Beta version

The present article will show you the steps on getting your hands on the latest version beta of SharinPix. You will get the chance to try out the freshly-developed features of the upcoming releases and gauge its impact on your business use cases.

## Install for Android devices

### 1. Join beta program

Join the SharinPix Beta program by clicking on the following URL within your Android device:

{% hint style="info" %}
[https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.sharinpix.SharinPix](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.sharinpix.SharinPix)
{% endhint %}

You will then be redirected to following page as shown below. Click on "**BECOME A TESTER** " to join the beta.

![](../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_2019-05-07-11-40-01_\(1\).png)

### 2. Install SharinPix Beta

You will receive the following screen, as shown below, once you click on "**BECOME A TESTER**"

![](../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_20190507-115934_\(1\).png)

After joining the beta you will have the possibility to:

* Update SharinPix to its latest beta version if you already have it installed on your device.
* Or, download the beta version of the SharinPix App directly from Google Play.

## Install for IOS devices

### 1. Join beta program

Join the SharinPix Beta program by clicking on the following URL within your IOS device:

{% hint style="info" %}
Ensure you have the TestFlight App already present on your IOS device. The click on the following URL:\
\
[https://testflight.apple.com/join/pM41PPi1](https://testflight.apple.com/join/pM41PPi1)
{% endhint %}

### 2. Install SharinPix Beta

Once you click on the above URL, the TestFlight app will launch and prompt you to join the beta and update the SharinPix app to its latest beta version.
