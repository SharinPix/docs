# SharinPix Mobile App: Access to Shooting Job

Shooting Jobs are categorisation of your assignments. Each shooting job can go in different SharinPix Album, that is, each shooting job can also be associated with specific Salesforce object(s).

* On the starting screen of your SharinPix app is listed all your Shooting Jobs
* Simply tap on the required job and start adding images
  * Images can be added from your gallery(roll) or by snapping camera images
