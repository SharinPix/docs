# SharinPix mobile app: Minimal Requirements

These minimum requirements follow Salesforce's recommended platform versions. Salesforce's current requirements take precedence if they differ from this page. [View Salesforce platform requirements](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.salesforce_app_requirements.htm\&type=5).

**iOS requirements:**

* Minimum iOS version: 18.0

**Android requirements:**

* Minimum Android version: 12.0
* Minimum internal storage: 16GB
* Minimum recommended RAM (memory): 1.5 GB
* Minimum Android WebView version: 90.0
* Tablets/phones with a camera supporting autofocus and flash. _Note: Some Android devices do not support cameras and can block SharinPix usage and many other camera-related apps._
* Preferred hardware models: Samsung/Pixel/Nexus (Other devices will work, but 100% compatibility with the device camera cannot be guaranteed).

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Note:**

* Salesforce's minimal requirements are subject to change at Salesforce's sole discretion, with or without advance notice.
* For any issues with the SharinPix mobile app, contact us as indicated [here](../getting-started-with-sharinpix/how-to-contact-support.md).
