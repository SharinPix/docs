# SharinPix mobile app: Minimal Requirements

**iOS requirements:**

* Minimum iOS version: 17.0

**Android requirements:**

* Minimum Android version: 10.0
* Minimum internal storage: 16GB
* Minimum recommended RAM (memory): 1.5 GB
* Tablets/phones with the camera supporting autofocus and flash. _Note: Some Android devices do not support cameras and can block SharinPix usage and many other camera-related apps._
* Preferred hardware models: Samsung/Pixel/Nexus (Other devices will work but 100% compatibility with the device camera cannot be guaranteed).
