# Community page refreshes when uploading multiple images to a SharinPix album. What should I do?

It can happen that your Community page refreshes when uploading multiple images onto a SharinPix Album at once. To resolve this, you should deactivate the **Auto Refresh View** option on your album component:

![](<.gitbook/assets/image (10).png>)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Information:**

Salesforce has depreciated the auto-refresh option and now recommends not to use the same within Salesforce Community. Therefore, we strongly advise to not enable the **Auto Refresh View** option on a SharinPix album added to a Community.
{% endhint %}
