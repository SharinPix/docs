# How to record the screen on Android?

If you are facing a problem on Android using Salesforce mobile App, Saloforce Field Service Lightning App or even SharinPix App, it could be very helpful to capture your screen to show us what your problem looks like.

To do so, please use one of those App as described in this blog post:

[https://www.guidingtech.com/36732/record-screen-android-lollipop/](https://www.guidingtech.com/36732/record-screen-android-lollipop/)
