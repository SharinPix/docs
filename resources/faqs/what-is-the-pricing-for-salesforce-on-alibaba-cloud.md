# What is the pricing for Salesforce on Alibaba cloud?

SharinPix pricing is currently coming with a specific pricing dedicated for Salesforce on Alibaba Cloud, made especially for customers in China.

## Salesforce on Alibaba Cloud

The price for this is 24USD per user per month. Please contact us for more details and access to the Alibaba Cloud Marketplace.

More details about our Pricing on Salesforce to be used outside of China can be found [here](what-is-the-pricing-for-sharinpix.md)
