# What should I do when I have to import files on SharinPix?

All bulk imports should be discussed with the SharinPix team first. If you are planning to import files in bulk to SharinPix, it's best to contact our support team at [support@sharinpix.com](mailto:support@sharinpix.com) first. This will ensure you do not exceed any applicable rate limits.

Rate limits are there to protect yourself and other customers. More information is available in our [SharinPix Fair Use Policy document](https://sharinpix.com/fair-usage-policy/).
